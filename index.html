AmarTaka Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

In short: everything you enter in AmarTaka stays on your device. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics, and the Android app does not have permission to use the internet. We, the developer, never receive your data.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the AmarTaka mobile app for Android and iOS ("the app"), published by the developer of AmarTaka ("we").

2. Data we collect

None. The app does not send any data to us or to anyone else. We run no server for the app and have no access to what you enter.

3. Data stored on your device

To work as a money manager, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • Your records: amounts, dates, notes, categories and accounts.
  • Your accounts, balances, budgets and recurring records.
  • Names you type for money you lent or borrowed, and optional due dates.
  • Your recent searches inside the app.
  • Your settings, such as currency, theme and reminder time.
  • If you set an app lock: a salted hash of your PIN, kept in the secure storage of your device (Android Keystore or iOS Keychain). The PIN itself is never stored.

Fingerprint and face unlock are handled by your device. The app only learns whether the check passed and never sees your biometric data.

The app is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup, so this data is not copied to your Google account by the system.

4. When data leaves the app

Data leaves the app only when you choose it:

  • Backup: you can save a backup file to your device, or to a folder you pick, by hand or on a schedule you turn on.
  • Export: you can save or share your records as a CSV or PDF file.
  • Share: you can send a backup or export to another app, such as Google Drive or email.

Once a file is saved or shared, it is outside the app. The service or app you send it to handles it under its own privacy policy. Backup and export files hold your financial records and may not be encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe.

5. Third parties

We do not sell, share or transfer your data. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking software.

6. Permissions

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or SMS.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete it at any time:

  • Use Settings › Reset app to erase all data in the app.
  • Uninstall the app to remove the app and its data from your device.

Backup and export files you saved elsewhere are not removed by these steps. Delete them where you stored them.

Because we hold none of your data, we cannot recover it for you. If you lose your device or reset the app without a backup, the data is gone.

8. Security

Your data is protected by the security of your device. You can add an app lock with a PIN and biometrics. While the lock is on, the app hides its content from screenshots and the recent-apps view.

9. Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone.

10. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR give you rights over personal data that an organisation holds about you. We hold no personal data from the app, so your data is fully under your own control on your device.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and its effective date before the change is released.
